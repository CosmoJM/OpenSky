# Word lists

- `answers.txt` — the daily words, in play order (one a day, then it starts over): everyday five-letter words from
  SCOWL sizes 10–35 (English + American), without simple plurals/past tenses and with words a family screen
  shouldn't show removed.
- `allowed.txt` — every word accepted as a guess: five-letter words from SCOWL sizes 10–70, plus the answers.

Made from SCOWL 2020.12.07 (http://wordlist.aspell.net/), used under its permissive licence. SCOWL's full copyright,
sources and credits (Kevin Atkinson; Moby Words II and 12Dicts — public domain; Ispell; WordNet; and others) are in
[`SCOWL-COPYRIGHT.txt`](SCOWL-COPYRIGHT.txt), which must stay with these files.

Add your own guess words (e.g. new slang) in opensky.json → `wordgame.extraWords`.
